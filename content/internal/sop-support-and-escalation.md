## Priorities and response targets
- Urgent: service down or data at risk for the whole business. Acknowledge within 30 minutes, work continuously until resolved, update the client every 2 hours.
- High: a key system degraded or a team blocked. Acknowledge within 2 business hours, update daily.
- Normal: one user affected or a workaround exists. Acknowledge within 1 business day.
- Low: questions and small changes. Acknowledge within 2 business days.
Response targets are internal goals. The contractual numbers are in each client's agreement; never quote targets to a client as a promise.

## Who handles what
- The account manager owns the relationship and every escalation for their clients.
- Any suspected security incident (ransomware, phishing that worked, unknown admin accounts, data leak) goes to the account manager immediately and to the security lead the same hour, whatever the stated priority.
- Enterprise accounts: any high or urgent ticket is handed to the account manager in person, not left in the queue.

## Working a ticket in Jira
1. Check the assistant reference (TK-...) and the client label on the issue.
2. Move the issue to In Progress when you start, and assign it to yourself.
3. Put client-visible progress in a comment that starts with "Client update:"; the website assistant reads the status, not internal comments.
4. Resolve only after the client confirms or after two follow-ups with no reply over 5 business days.

## Communication
Write to clients in plain language: what happened, what we did, what happens next, when they will hear from us again. No blame, no guesses about cause before the investigation is done.
