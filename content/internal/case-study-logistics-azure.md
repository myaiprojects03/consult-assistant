## Situation
A logistics company with two ageing on-premises server rooms, frequent outages during peak season and no disaster recovery plan.

## What we did
- Assessment of 60 servers; retired 18, rebuilt 9 as managed services, moved the rest to Azure.
- Landing zone with hub-and-spoke networking, Azure Policy guardrails and cost alerts per department.
- Backup and disaster recovery with a tested 4-hour recovery target for the transport management system.

## Results
- No unplanned outages in the 12 months after the move (previously 7 a year).
- Infrastructure running costs down 22 percent after reserved instances.
- Recovery test passed in 2 hours 40 minutes.
