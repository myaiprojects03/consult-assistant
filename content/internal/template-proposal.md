## Structure
1. Summary: the client's goal in their words, and the outcome we will deliver.
2. Current situation: what we found in discovery; risks if nothing changes.
3. Approach: phases, each with deliverables and an acceptance check.
4. Timeline: by phase, with the assumptions it depends on.
5. Team: named account manager and lead consultant.
6. Investment: fixed price per phase, or day rate with an estimate range.
7. Assumptions and exclusions.
8. Next steps: sign-off, kickoff date, first workshop.

## Pricing guidance (internal, 2026)
- Standard consultant day rate: GBP 850. Senior or architect: GBP 1,100.
- Discovery and assessment: fixed price, usually 3 to 5 days of effort.
- Microsoft 365 migration: priced per mailbox band; under 50 users is usually 8 to 12 days.
- Managed services: monthly per user, from GBP 45 for support only, GBP 70 with security monitoring.
- Discounts above 10 percent need director approval.
Never put a price in a proposal without the scoped effort behind it.

## Tone
Short sentences, the client's vocabulary, no jargon without a one-line explanation. Every claim about results needs a case study or a reference we can name with permission.
